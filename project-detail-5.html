PeopleU: Subscription Pricing and Promotional Effectiveness Analysis

Project Details

Duration: 10 Days
Team: Individual
Tools: SQL, Tableau

*Disclaimer: Subscription Pricing Analysis + SaaS project is a part of RevoU Data Insight Project, a 4-week case study designed to analyze customized datasets and address business challenges. Supervised by RevoU, this project hones analytical skills, strengthens problem-solving abilities, and guides students in delivering actionable insights.

Overview

Business Background

PeopleU, a B2B SaaS HR management platform, expanded its promotional policy in 2022 to an aggressive flat 20% discount across all tiers to drive customer acquisition volume. While total contract volume grew from 347 to 554, this unsegmented approach caused severe margin cannibalization, declining promo ROI, and heavy promotional dependency. To ensure sustainable growth, PeopleU needed to evaluate price elasticity and unit economics across tiers to eliminate revenue leakage and recapture up to +10% (+Rp 140.2M) in total revenue uplift.

Method

  • Processed and validated historical records across 1,754 contract transactions, lead databases, and discount usage logs.
  • Calculated Price Elasticity of Demand (PED) per tier and analyzed unit economics to detect per-seat price burdens.
  • Evaluated multi-year cohort retention rates and tier-movement patterns to assess post-discount churn risks.

Result

  • Discovered Enterprise demand is organic with 59.86% converting at full price (making 20% discounts wasteful), whereas Business tier faced an 18x higher per-seat cost burden (Rp 1,021.69 vs Rp 55.92/seat), driving 63.89% promo dependency.
  • Confirmed strong product loyalty with >89% retention across all acquisition types and 99.2% same-tier renewals, proving discount removals carry minimal churn risk.
  • Recommended setting Enterprise and Startup discounts to 0% and capping Business discounts at 10%, projecting a total revenue uplift of +10.93% (+Rp 140.2M, expanding revenue from Rp 1.28B to Rp 1.428B).
