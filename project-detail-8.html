RevoU x Telkom Indonesia: Residential Broadband Customer Churn Prediction

Project Details

Duration: 10 Days
Team: Individual
Tools: Python (Scikit-Learn, XGBoost, SHAP)

Overview

Business Background

Telkom Indonesia faced a 10% monthly churn rate among residential broadband customers, resulting in Rp 174.18M in monthly revenue loss. Because acquiring new customers is far more expensive than keeping existing ones, this project aimed to predict churn early and protect customer lifetime value (LTV).

Method

  • Analyzed 20,000 customer records to identify top churn drivers, such as service outages and payment delays.
  • Trained and compared Machine Learning models (Logistic Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost) handling a 10% imbalanced dataset.
  • Selected XGBoost as the best model for capturing 88% of actual churners while minimizing false alarms.
  • Applied SHAP model explainability to group customers into clear Risk Tiers (High, Medium, Low) for targeted retention.

Result

  • Achieved 88.0% Recall and 0.914 PR-AUC with the XGBoost model to reliably flag high-risk subscribers.
  • Found that service downtime (>2 hours/month) and month-to-month contracts are the biggest churn triggers, concentrated heavily in 5 main districts.
  • Focusing on the top 20.75% high-risk users protects Rp 139.38M/month (80% of total revenue loss).
  • Recommended priority technician dispatch for high-risk areas, automated billing reminders, and contract upgrade incentives.
