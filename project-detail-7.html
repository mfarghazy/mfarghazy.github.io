RevoU x Telkom Indonesia: Residential Broadband Customer Analytics Dashboard

Project Details

Duration: 10 Days
Team: Individual
Tools: Python, Spreadsheet, Tableau

*Disclaimer: Residential Broadband Customer Analytics Dashboard for Telecommunication industry project is a part of RevoU x Telkom Indonesia Virtual Internship Project. The data provided is a synthetic representation created for educational purposes. It does not represent actual Telkom Indonesia customer data, operational performance, financial information, or business conditions. Any similarities to real-world situations are purely coincidental.

Overview

Business Background

Telkom Indonesia faced a 10% customer churn rate across DKI Jakarta residential broadband customers, resulting in Rp 513.40M in lost revenue. Due to fragmented data monitoring, business teams struggled to quickly isolate downtime issues, contract vulnerabilities, and high-risk customer segments.

Method

  • Processed operational and subscriber datasets across DKI Jakarta districts using Python in Google Colab.
  • Conducted exploratory data analysis (EDA) to map churn against network downtime duration, ticket resolution speeds, contract types, device density, and payment behavior.
  • Designed a 3-tab interactive Tableau dashboard specifically structured for Executive, Network Operations, and Retention managers to monitor key performance indicators and high-risk customer segments.

Result

  • Identified network quality issues as the main cancellation cause (43.45% of total churn), driven by average monthly downtime of 3.2 hours and resolution times averaging 4.4 days.
  • Discovered churn peaks at Months 4–6 (34% of churn exposure)—primarily among short-term monthly contract holders—as well as speed friction among 508 households on basic 50 Mbps plans connecting 3–4 devices simultaneously.
  • Formulated targeted field SLA dispatch plans for top-risk districts (Penjaringan, Cilincing, Cakung, Koja, Kalideres), Month-3 contract migration incentives, and automated 75/100 Mbps trial upgrades to safeguard Rp 642.07M in revenue at risk.
